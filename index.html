A practical starting point

The right starting point is usually the work itself.

Statum is a software engineering company based in Nairobi, Kenya. We work with clients here and around the world to replace workarounds, connect the systems they already use and make day-to-day work easier.

Sometimes that means an internal tool. Sometimes it is a customer portal, a mobile app or a connection between systems that do not currently share information. We spend time understanding the work before deciding what to build.
